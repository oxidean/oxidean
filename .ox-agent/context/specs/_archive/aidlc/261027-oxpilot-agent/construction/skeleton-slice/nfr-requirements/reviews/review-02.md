### Summary

The skeleton NFR set is mostly faithful to upstream and the fail-closed/fail-safe pins check out against the repo, but three Majors block readiness: a cluster of requirements (audit, logging, cascade, hook seam) presumes a session-delete surface that BR1.1 and the U1 boundary exclude — including one seam that is contractually U2's; and NFR1.8's cornerstone redaction mechanism rests on an "existing secrets-pattern filter" that does not exist, masking that U1 must author it. Minors cover a mandatory-vs-optional audit contradiction, a broadcast-lag hole in the exactly-once claim, unmeasurable p99 framing, an understated DoS surface, the un-pinned plaintext-storage disclosure, and a drifted error-code name.
