### Review
All ten iteration-2 findings verified adversarially against the current artifacts — the fixes are real, not cosmetic, and both Majors are fully closed in the normative text. R-06: entities.md §Target constraints now pins new-row-per-pair verbatim and agrees with BR6.1/WF4. R-27: BR6.5 + WF5 flip session-scoped grants and queued/claimed/running actions →`cancelled` directly, pin the cascade-deletion rationale, audit `session_deleted`, and state the report→`not_found` kill-locally consequence; entities.md §Action constraints agree. The eight Minors (R-28..R-35) are each present and correctly worded. The fix pass introduced no new Majors but left a cluster of Minor defects — largest class: `functional-design-questions.md` never re-synced (Q5/Q7/Q8/Q9 still carry pre-amendment/pre-fix text including the exact undeliverable-delivery claim R-27 removed). Five smaller stale/overstated lines + two unpinned spots (BR5.1's "connector's own timeout closes it" unimplementable; BR7.2 audit detail lacks `reason`; the dispatch error-channel union unpinned). The stranded `claimed`/`running` residual is declared, visible via `action_status`, and closed by server-side paths — Accepted risk.
