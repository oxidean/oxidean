### Summary

Iteration-1's fixes landed faithfully across all six artifacts — the C7 signature, the `connector_id := target_id` identity, the two-arm dequeue re-check, the dequeue-before-park ordering, BR3.1 lazy expiry at both consent observes, the mint-gate caps bound + per-user limiter + `/pair` carve-out, `record_with`/`resolve_enablement_slot` on the C3 surface, entity-verbatim columns, post-commit audit placement, kind-branched deny events, and the flagged `verifier_hash` NULLABLE deviation all check out against ground truth. The corrections introduced one real defect (R-27): `grant_respond`'s allow arm stamps `g.kind` before the connect/action test, which overwrites connect to `action_once`, materializes an Action from NULL args, and never flips the target `connected` — the only target-activation path. Seven minors accompany it: an `allowed→expired` stamp outside entities' closed transitions, a subscribe-order lost-wake window, a missing `action_grant_pending` site row and an unmapped `forbidden` redeem event, `cancel_requested` absent from disconnect's direct-cancel set, a check-only mint limiter, dedupe on un-normalized args, and `body.requested_caps`/`AppState.oxpilot.runtime` name drifts. Fix the Major and close the minors — the design is otherwise implementable.
