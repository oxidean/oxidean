### Summary

The entity model is faithful to the shipped U1 schema and the scoping and redaction posture is sound, but the design cannot be built as written: it contradicts the pinned C1/C2 queue contract (auto-enqueue plus missing message_queue and renamed queue_list), plants a concrete service type inside an api-core struct that creates a crate cycle, and leaves the tool-call identity model, message-status vocabulary, and budget semantics unresolved against shipped code and the contract file. Six Majors plus one Critical — implementable only after contract reconciliation and the corrections above.
