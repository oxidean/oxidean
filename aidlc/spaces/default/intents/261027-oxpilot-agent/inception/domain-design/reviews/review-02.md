### Summary

The catalogue skeleton is in good shape: eight uniquely-named components with symmetric depends_on/dependents, an acyclic graph, each entity owned exactly once, all declared references resolving, and every US1.1–US5.5 row mapped to a plausible carrier. OxpilotApi's scope (procedures + SSE/connector routes + gates + audit) is defensible against the repo's api-rest/handler precedent, and ConnectorGateway is buildable in principle on the runner claim/report pattern. The NOT-READY verdict rests on the consent-critical surface, where the two deliverables contradict each other (R-01 transport/auth, R-03 consent modelling/state drift), a stated security behaviour is undeliverable as designed (R-02 in-flight revoke), and invariant ownership is ambiguous exactly where this stage is supposed to fix it (R-04 budgets, R-06 delete-vs-turn/cascade). The five majors are all stage-level fixes — entity additions, owner naming, and reconciling the ADRs with the catalogue — not rework of the decomposition itself; resolving them here is much cheaper than letting contract-design guess. The minors are clarification-sized.
