### Summary

The layering is sound (trait-in-api-core, impl-in-api-oxpilot, one-way edges) and the deep-concurrency questions (dedupe arms, drain atomicity, hook ordering) are thought through — but the budget gate's config source is fabricated (`slot.resolved` does not exist and the service can't reach the slot anyway), the `message_queue` error code contradicts the C1 pin, the trait is missing verbs the fd spec mandates, `delete_session_cascade` is called but never planned, the 0087 schema contradicts entities.md on two tables, and the C2 snapshot composition (`pending_queue` + tool_calls) fell out of every seam delta. Fix the Critical + Majors and this is implementable.
