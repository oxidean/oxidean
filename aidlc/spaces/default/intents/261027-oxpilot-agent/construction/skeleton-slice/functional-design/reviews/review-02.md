### Summary

The skeleton's boundary and registration/enablement claims check out against the repo (fail-closed SessionOnly, dispatch fan-out, route-registration triangle, MCP-flag pattern), but the design is not implementable as written: the persisted entity's FK types contradict the live schema, a declared reference targets a U6-owned table that doesn't exist yet, and the two core mechanisms of the chat round-trip — the active-turn registry behind `409 turn_active`/snapshot `active_turn` and the rule that decides when the provider slot holds the stub vs `None` — are unspecified. Fix the four Major items and the entity/spec minors; the traceability ledger also needs the missing AC rows and the AC5.1.4 mis-map corrected.
