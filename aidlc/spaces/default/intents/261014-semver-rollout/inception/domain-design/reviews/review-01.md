### Summary

The decomposition is sound — eleven coherent components, an acyclic depends_on graph, registry-DAG semantics matching the real Cargo/Bun graph (api→core/db/git, cli→core, db→core, web→api-client, api→api-client generated edge, wire→api/cli/api-client/web/web-ts), and well-formed ADRs that correctly capture the credential/tag-cascade trap. Three defects block a clean handoff: the SemVer compatibility rule the requirements explicitly assigned to this stage was never decided (R-01), the OpenAPI `info.version` surface required by FR6.4 is assigned to no component (R-02), and the machine-readable catalogue violates its own stated well-formedness rules (R-03). All are small, bounded revisions rather than redesigns.
