### Notes

- Verified against rules.md: every BR id cited exists and means what the rows claim. The high-risk content survived translation faithfully — BR3.2's SSRF bound (literal IP notations + DNS-resolve-all-public + resolved-provider keying + documented TOCTOU + Ollama exception + provider-less shape check) is restated at full strength in NFR1.8–1.11; the R-06 retry fix (connect-phase-only, sent-but-unanswered never retries) lands correctly in NFR4.8/4.10; the R-14 audit-ordering fix lands in NFR5.3; R-01/R-04 build-before-persist + mutex serialization in NFR4.1–4.2; R-12/R-19 provider-less-write and flag/budget exemptions in NFR4.3–4.4.
- Custody claims verified against crates/oxidean-api-core/src/crypto.rs + actions/secrets.rs: AES-256-GCM `encrypt`/`decrypt` under a caller-chosen env chain is exactly the shipped pattern; `["OXIDEAN_OXPILOT_SECRETS_KEY","OXIDEAN_SESSION_SECRET"]` matches the actions-secrets KEY_ENVS precedent, and `crypto.unavailable` correctly mirrors key_bytes' fail-closed behaviour. Error vocabulary (auth.unauthenticated, auth.pat_scope, admin.forbidden/auth.banned, rpc.bad_input, invalid_provider, crypto.unavailable) matches shipped codes (admin.rs, bearer.rs, gate.rs) and BR5.1 — the R-17 drift is fixed.
- Requirements are predominantly verifiable invariants rather than design restatements (no per-request DB read, O(1) swap, bounded 10s/300s timeouts, field-names-only audit, wiremock-only) — good.
- Cross-doc duplicates (NFR1.5/NFR4.13 key-pair atomicity, NFR1.9/NFR4.9 redirects, NFR1.7/NFR5.2 value-free audit) restate the same invariant under two NFR domains — acceptable, keep them textually identical.
- Nitpicks not worth findings: reliability-requirements.md header lists WF2–WF3 but NFR4.10 cites WF4; NFR3.6's "one settings-row read + one adapter construction" understates the write path (persist write + audit insert also occur); NFR3.15's replica-convergence bound is new-but-consistent with the single-replica/documented-caveat spirit of upstream NFR4.
- Blocking item is R-01 only: the traceability ledger must not launder tech-stack/test-isolation rows as accessibility/usability coverage; renumber or re-disposition before approval.
