## Review

U3's design is unusually well-anchored in the shipped plumbing: the admission order (BR1.1) matches `chat_post` as built (`routes.rs:222-496`), durable-first (BR2.4) is consistent with the C2 snapshot-merge model, the ADR-010 per-turn provider pin and C8 hook ordering are honored, and the residual-drain placement (BR4.2) correctly keeps `seq` temporal. The failures concentrate exactly where the unit adds new machinery: the drain→chain pipeline has a commit-window hole that silently strands a just-queued row; the flagged C4 `ChatMsg` amendment provably does not carry what the Ollama wire and the durable card key require; the panic-release guarantee names a mechanism that does not deliver it; and terminal emission has three writers with no first-wins rule. Several smaller pins (error-mapping arm, chained-turn `ActiveTurn` seeding, adapter coalescing, counting semantics) are missing and force the implementer to guess.
