| R-02 | Major | reliability-design.md §REL-3 (lines 49–66) and nfr-design-questions.md Q2-A | "Any other probe outcome (other codes, probe throw, non-2xx) => nothing — native reconnect continues" is verifiably false for the entire HTTP-denial class. Per the WHATWG SSE processing model, any non-200 status (or a non-`text/event-stream` content type) permanently fails the connection — readyState CLOSED, no reconnect; only network errors reestablish. The route maps `forbidden` to 403, `not_found` to 404, `budget_exceeded` to 429, and everything else to 500 `oxpilot.internal`; session auth emits `auth.unauthenticated` as 401. So mid-visit auth expiry, `forbidden`, `budget_exceeded`, `oxpilot.internal`, or any deploy-window 5xx on a reconnect attempt kills the EventSource permanently while the probe resolves to "nothing" — a silently dead stream that contradicts the design's own "never a dead spinner" contract and NFR4.6's every-negative-path-renders-a-typed-notice pin. `es.readyState` is readable inside `onerror` (CONNECTING vs CLOSED) but the design never uses it. | Extend the probe verdict: on any non-fatal outcome, check `es.readyState === CLOSED` (or handle `auth.*`/`forbidden`/`oxpilot.internal` probe results explicitly) and render the typed surface — e.g. an auth-denial routed through the existing auth gate, otherwise a stream-lost banner — and scope the "native reconnect continues to the next snapshot" claim to transport drops only. | Open |
